A plus plushie (js/plushie.js): a toy, not a monster, so it has plush tells (a stitched seam, button eyes, a sewn smile, felt specks, a heart patch, a tag) and never moves on its own. It sits by the captain on the start scene and squeaks when tapped. On each wall, then at 72, 40 and 26 px.
