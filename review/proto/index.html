Sumgeon prototype

Two new kinds of problem to try on a phone before they go in the game: telling time with Tick-Tockus, and what comes next with Slimelipsis. Nothing is saved or learned here. The voice is the device's own (these lines aren't recorded yet); the small line under the problem is what it says.

Type the answer and tap Hit! (or Enter). 💡 (or ?) shows the hint; a miss shows the answer and the worked hint. Next (or Enter again) brings a new problem.
