Sumgeon prototype

New kinds of problem to try on a phone before they go in the game, one per icon: time, patterns, shapes, true or false, the number line, place value, dollars, and measuring length with Rulerpillar (📏, still a prototype). Nothing is saved or learned here. The voice is the device's own (these lines aren't recorded yet); the small line under the problem is what it says.

📊 Charts: Graphzilla's charts, a kind for each grade from 1st to 5th (tally charts, picture and bar graphs, scaled ones, line plots, frequency tables with mode and range, the median, line graphs). ?type=chart&grade=3 opens a grade, &level=sbar3 a kind, &q=m a question (how many more), &chart=<key> one fact.

Type the answer and tap Hit! (or Enter). 💡 (or ?) shows the hint; a miss shows the answer and the worked hint. Next (or Enter again) brings a new problem.
