What you're approving: a quiet background ambience for each stratum, made live in the browser (no recordings), so it never loops: its drips, chords, chimes and bells come on a slow random schedule. Nothing is in the game yet. Listen to each one for a few minutes, ideally on the iPhone and iPad, then mark it ✓ Sounds good or ⚑ Something's wrong.

Once it's wired in, music will be off by default with its own switch in Settings → Game (apart from sound effects and voice), at the level you leave the slider on here, and it will duck under the voice. Voice over it plays a recorded game line and ducks the music, so you can hear that.
